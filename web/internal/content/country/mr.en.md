# Mauritania

This section is published in English and Arabic. If they differ, the English text applies.

This section applies if you use KIFF or tafs in Mauritania. It adds what Law no. 2017-020 on the Protection of Personal Data ask, beyond KIFF's Privacy Policy and the tafs Privacy Policy. Where this section says more, it applies; for everything else, those two policies apply.

## 1. Who is responsible

Kiff Agents OÜ, a company in Estonia, is the data controller of your account and sign-in data. For what tafs keeps for your company (each taf's runs, chats, reports and files), your company is the controller and we act as its processor. Sections 3 and 4 of each policy say which is which.

## 2. What we process and why

What we process, why, on what legal basis and for how long is in sections 4, 5 and 8 of KIFF's policy and sections 4, 8 and 9 of the tafs policy. We process your data to run your account and your tafs, to keep the service secure, and to meet our legal duties. Where a consent is the basis, such as for the AI in tafs, we ask for it separately and you can withdraw it in Account.

## 3. Transfers outside Mauritania

KIFF is run from Estonia and hosted in Ireland. Everything you send us leaves Mauritania. Some recipients are in the United States, as section 7 of each policy lists. Each transfer rests on the safeguards section 7 of KIFF's policy names.

## 4. Your rights

You can ask to see your data, to object to its processing, to have it corrected or deleted, and to withdraw a consent you gave. Write to privacy@kiff.dev. We answer within 30 days.

## 5. Children

KIFF and tafs are not for anyone under 16.

## 6. Declaration

**Declaration.** The law asks a person who processes personal data to declare it to the personal data protection authority, or to ask for its authorisation. We have made no declaration today. We will make it, and say so here, before KIFF and tafs are offered in Mauritania.

## 7. Where to complain

You can complain to the personal data protection authority of Mauritania.
