# Taiwan

This section is published in English and Traditional Chinese. If they differ, the English text applies.

This section applies if you use KIFF or tafs in Taiwan. It adds what the Personal Data Protection Act asks, beyond KIFF's Privacy Policy and the tafs Privacy Policy. Where this section says more, it applies; for everything else, those two policies apply.

## 1. Who is responsible

Kiff Agents OÜ, a company in Estonia, is the data controller of your account and sign-in data. For what tafs keeps for your company (each taf's runs, chats, reports and files), your company is the controller and we act as its processor. Sections 3 and 4 of each policy say which is which.

## 2. What we tell you when we collect your data

When we collect your data we tell you who we are, why we collect it, what kind of data it is, for how long and where it is used, who uses it and how, your rights, and what happens if you don't give it. Sections 1, 4, 5, 8 and 9 of KIFF's policy and sections 1, 4, 8, 9 and 10 of the tafs policy do that.

## 3. Transfers outside Taiwan

KIFF is run from Estonia and hosted in Ireland. Everything you send us leaves Taiwan. Some recipients are in the United States, as section 7 of each policy lists. Each transfer rests on the safeguards section 7 of KIFF's policy names. The authority in charge may restrict a transfer of personal data abroad, and we follow any such order.

## 4. Your rights

You can ask to be told how your data is processed, to see it, to have a copy of it, to have it added to or corrected, to have its collection, processing or use stopped, and to have it deleted. Write to privacy@kiff.dev. We answer within 15 days of a request to see it or to have a copy, and within 30 days of any other request.

## 5. Children

KIFF and tafs are not for anyone under 16.

## 6. Representative

Taiwan's law does not ask for a representative in Taiwan, and we have none. Our privacy contact is privacy@kiff.dev.

## 7. Where to complain

You can complain to the authority in charge or to your local government.
