# Moldova

This section is published in English and Romanian. If they differ, the English text applies.

This section applies if you use KIFF or tafs in the Republic of Moldova. It adds what Law no. 195/2024 on the protection of personal data asks, beyond KIFF's Privacy Policy and the tafs Privacy Policy. Where this section says more, it applies; for everything else, those two policies apply.

## 1. Who is responsible

Kiff Agents OÜ, a company in Estonia, is the data controller of your account and sign-in data. For what tafs keeps for your company (each taf's runs, chats, reports and files), your company is the controller and we act as its processor. Sections 3 and 4 of each policy say which is which.

## 2. What we process and why

What we process, why, on what legal basis and for how long is in sections 4, 5, 8 and 9 of each policy. We process your data to run your account and your tafs, to keep the service secure, and to meet our legal duties. Where a consent is the basis, such as for the AI in tafs, we ask for it separately and you can withdraw it in Account.

## 3. Transfers outside Moldova

KIFF is run from Estonia and hosted in Ireland. Everything you send us leaves Moldova. Some recipients are in the United States, as section 7 of each policy lists. The law lets personal data go abroad to a country that gives adequate protection, which includes the member states of the European Union, or on safeguards such as the standard contract of the National Centre for Personal Data Protection. We transfer your data on the safeguards section 7 of each policy names, and we keep the contracts that show them.

## 4. Your rights

You can ask to see your data and to have a copy of it, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to restrict its processing, to receive it in a portable form, to object to its processing, to withdraw a consent you gave, and not to be subject to a decision made only by automated processing that has legal effect on you. Write to privacy@kiff.dev. We answer within 30 days at the latest, and sooner where the law sets a shorter time.

## 5. Children

KIFF and tafs are not for anyone under 16.

## 6. Representative

**Representative in Moldova.** A controller outside Moldova that offers services to people in Moldova appoints a representative there in writing, unless it is established in a member state of the European Economic Area, as the National Centre's guidance reads the law. Kiff Agents OÜ is established in Estonia, so we have no representative in Moldova. If that changes, we will name one here.

## 7. Where to complain

You can complain to the National Centre for Personal Data Protection of the Republic of Moldova (Centrul Național pentru Protecția Datelor cu Caracter Personal, CNPDCP).
