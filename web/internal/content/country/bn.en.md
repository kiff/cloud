# Brunei

This section is published in English only. English is the language of the law's text and meets the rule for Brunei.

This section applies if you use KIFF or tafs in Brunei Darussalam. It adds what the Personal Data Protection Order 2025 (PDPO), in force since 1 January 2026, asks about sending personal data abroad, beyond KIFF's Privacy Policy and the tafs Privacy Policy. Where this section says more, it applies; for everything else, those two policies apply.

## 1. Who is responsible

Kiff Agents OÜ, a company in Estonia, is the data controller of your account and sign-in data. For what tafs keeps for your company (each taf's runs, chats, reports and files), your company is the controller and we act as its processor. Section 3 of each policy says which is which.

## 2. Transfers outside Brunei

KIFF is run from Estonia and hosted in Ireland. Everything you send us leaves Brunei. Some recipients are in the United States, as section 7 of KIFF's policy lists (the tafs policy's section 7 points to it). The PDPO lets personal data go abroad only where the receiving country, or the contract with the recipient, gives protection comparable to the Order's, as the Authority for Info-communications Technology Industry of Brunei Darussalam (AITI) reads it. We transfer your data to Estonia, Ireland and the United States on the safeguards section 7 of KIFF's policy names: the standard contractual clauses of the European Union, supplementary measures, and, for United States recipients that have certified, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. We keep the contracts that show them.

## 3. Your rights

You can ask to see your data, to have a copy of it, and to have it corrected, and you can withdraw a consent you gave. Section 9 of KIFF's policy and section 10 of the tafs policy list the rest of your rights. Write to privacy@kiff.dev. We answer within 30 days at the latest, and sooner where the law sets a shorter time.

## 4. Children

KIFF and tafs are not for anyone under 16.

## 5. Where to complain

You can complain to AITI, which administers the PDPO.
