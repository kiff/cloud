# Serbia

This section is published in English and Serbian (Latin script). If they differ, the English text applies.

This section applies if you use KIFF or tafs in Serbia. It adds what Serbia's Law on Personal Data Protection (Official Gazette no. 87/2018) asks, beyond KIFF's Privacy Policy and the tafs Privacy Policy. Where this section says more, it applies; for everything else, those two policies apply.

## 1. What we process, why, and how long

What we process, why, and for how long is in sections 4, 5 and 8 of each policy. In short: your account, your devices, what you write to Coach and your tafs and what your connected apps return while a taf works, billing details on a paid plan, and request logs. We delete each when its period ends, or when you delete your account.

## 2. Transfers outside Serbia

KIFF is run by Kiff Agents OÜ, a company in Estonia, and hosted in Ireland. Everything you send us leaves Serbia. Some recipients are in the United States: Clerk (sign-in), Anthropic (the AI models behind Coach and each taf), Stripe (payment on a paid plan), Resend (email notices), Apple and Google (push alerts and purchases), Cloudflare and Google Workspace (mail to a kiff.dev address) and Composio (managed connections). Each transfer is protected by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, as section 7 of each policy says.

## 3. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, object to or restrict its processing, ask for it in a portable form, withdraw a consent you gave, and not be subject to a decision made only by automated processing that has legal effect on you. Write to privacy@kiff.dev. We answer within one month.

## 4. Children

KIFF and tafs are not for anyone under 16.

## 5. Privacy contact and representative

Privacy contact: the managing director, Kiff Agents OÜ, privacy@kiff.dev.

**Representative in Serbia.** The law asks a controller outside Serbia that offers goods or services to people in Serbia, or monitors them, to appoint a representative in Serbia in writing. We have no representative in Serbia today. We will name one here, as the contact point for the Commissioner and for people in Serbia, before KIFF and tafs are offered in Serbia.

## 6. Where to complain

You can complain to the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection of Serbia (Poverenik za informacije od javnog značaja i zaštitu podataka o ličnosti).
