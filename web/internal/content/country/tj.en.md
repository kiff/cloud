# Tajikistan

This section is published in English and Tajik. If they differ, the English text applies.

This section applies if you use KIFF or tafs in the Republic of Tajikistan. It adds what the Law on Personal Data Protection, No. 1537 of 3 August 2018, asks about sending personal data abroad, beyond KIFF's Privacy Policy and the tafs Privacy Policy. Where this section says more, it applies; for everything else, those two policies apply.

## 1. Who is responsible

Kiff Agents OÜ, a company in Estonia, is the data controller of your account and sign-in data. For what tafs keeps for your company (each taf's runs, chats, reports and files), your company is the controller and we act as its processor. Section 3 of each policy says which is which.

## 2. Transfers outside Tajikistan

KIFF is run from Estonia and hosted in Ireland. Everything you send us leaves Tajikistan. Some recipients are in the United States, as section 7 of each policy lists. The law lets personal data go abroad to a country that gives adequate protection, and otherwise with the person's consent or under a treaty that Tajikistan recognises. We transfer your data on the safeguards section 7 of KIFF's policy names. Where the law asks for your consent to a transfer, creating an account and using KIFF or tafs is your agreement to your data being processed in Estonia, Ireland and the United States, and you can withdraw it by deleting your account or writing to privacy@kiff.dev.

## 3. Your rights

You can ask to see your data, to have a copy of it, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, and to withdraw a consent you gave. Section 9 of KIFF's policy and section 10 of the tafs policy list the rest of your rights. Write to privacy@kiff.dev. We answer within 30 days at the latest, and sooner where the law sets a shorter time.

## 4. Children

KIFF and tafs are not for anyone under 16.

## 5. Where to complain

You can complain to the authority that enforces the Law on Personal Data Protection in Tajikistan.
