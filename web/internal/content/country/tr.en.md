# Türkiye

This section is published in English and Turkish. If they differ, the English text applies.

This section applies if you use KIFF or tafs in Türkiye. It adds what Law no. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK) asks, beyond KIFF's Privacy Policy and the tafs Privacy Policy. Where this section says more, it applies; for everything else, those two policies apply.

## 1. Who is responsible

Kiff Agents OÜ, a company in Estonia, is the data controller of your account and sign-in data. For what tafs keeps for your company (each taf's runs, chats, reports and files), your company is the controller and we act as its processor. Sections 3 and 4 of each policy say which is which.

## 2. What we process and why

What we process, why, on what legal basis and for how long is in sections 4, 5 and 8 of KIFF's policy and sections 4, 8 and 9 of the tafs policy. These are the information the KVKK asks us to give you when we collect your data (Art. 10). We process your data to run your account and your tafs, to keep the service secure, and to meet our legal duties. Where a consent is the basis, such as for the AI in tafs, we ask for it separately and you can withdraw it in Account.

## 3. Transfers outside Türkiye

KIFF is run from Estonia and hosted in Ireland. Everything you send us leaves Türkiye. Some recipients are in the United States, as section 7 of each policy lists. Under Article 9 of the KVKK a transfer abroad needs an adequacy decision, appropriate safeguards (for example a standard contract that the Authority is told of), or one of the exceptional cases, such as your explicit consent. We transfer your data on the safeguards section 7 of KIFF's policy names, and we keep the contracts that show them.

## 4. Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK you can ask whether we process your data, ask for the details, ask what it is used for, ask who receives it at home or abroad, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete or destroy it, ask us to tell those who received it of any correction or deletion, object to a result against you reached only by automated analysis, and ask for compensation for loss caused by unlawful processing. Write to privacy@kiff.dev. We answer within 30 days, free of charge, unless the request needs a cost.

## 5. Children

KIFF and tafs are not for anyone under 16.

## 6. Representative and registry

**Representative in Türkiye.** A controller outside Türkiye registers in the data controllers' registry (VERBİS) through a representative in Türkiye. We have no representative in Türkiye today. We will name one here, as the contact point for the Personal Data Protection Authority and for people in Türkiye, before KIFF and tafs are offered in Türkiye.

## 7. Where to complain

First write to us at privacy@kiff.dev (Article 13 of the KVKK). If we refuse, your answer is inadequate or we don't answer within 30 days, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Board (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu), through the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK), under Article 14: within 30 days of our answer, and in any case within 60 days of your request.
