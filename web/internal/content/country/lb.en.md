# Lebanon

This section is published in English and Arabic. If they differ, the English text applies.

This section applies if you use KIFF or tafs in Lebanon. It adds what Law no. 81 of 2018 on Electronic Transactions and Personal Data ask, beyond KIFF's Privacy Policy and the tafs Privacy Policy. Where this section says more, it applies; for everything else, those two policies apply.

## 1. Who is responsible

Kiff Agents OÜ, a company in Estonia, is the data controller of your account and sign-in data. For what tafs keeps for your company (each taf's runs, chats, reports and files), your company is the controller and we act as its processor. Sections 3 and 4 of each policy say which is which.

## 2. What we process and why

What we process, why, on what legal basis and for how long is in sections 4, 5 and 8 of KIFF's policy and sections 4, 8 and 9 of the tafs policy. We process your data to run your account and your tafs, to keep the service secure, and to meet our legal duties. Where a consent is the basis, such as for the AI in tafs, we ask for it separately and you can withdraw it in Account.

## 3. Transfers outside Lebanon

KIFF is run from Estonia and hosted in Ireland. Everything you send us leaves Lebanon. Some recipients are in the United States, as section 7 of each policy lists. Each transfer rests on the safeguards section 7 of each policy names.

## 4. Your rights

You can ask to see your data, to have it corrected, updated or deleted, to object to its processing for a legitimate reason, and to withdraw a consent you gave. Write to privacy@kiff.dev. We answer within 30 days.

## 5. Children

KIFF and tafs are not for anyone under 16.

## 6. Declaration

**Declaration.** The law asks a person who processes personal data to declare it to the Ministry of Economy and Trade, unless the law exempts it, for example where the person has agreed in advance, or for a business's own clients' data within what it needs to run its activity. We process your data on the bases in sections 4, 5 and 8 of KIFF's policy and sections 4, 8 and 9 of the tafs policy (to run your account and your tafs, to keep the service secure, and to meet our legal duties), and ask for consent separately where it is the basis, such as for the AI in tafs. We have made no declaration today. If the ministry asks for one, we will make it before KIFF and tafs are offered in Lebanon, and say so here.

## 7. Where to complain

You can complain to the Ministry of Economy and Trade of Lebanon.
