# Approving on your phone

KIFF accepts an answer to a held call only with a signature made on your phone or iPad.
For anyone who approves in the tafs app.
A stolen sign-in can't approve anything: it has no key.

## Two keys

The app holds two keys in the device's secure hardware. The **approval key** signs only what
grants authority, and the key itself requires Face ID or Touch ID (or your passcode). The
**device key** signs everything else.

## What asks for Face ID or Touch ID

- **Approve once** and **Extra room**;
- confirming a draft, which issues a Card or starts a [test version](/docs/tafs-try-a-version);
- **Resume** ([Halt and resume](/docs/tafs-halt)).

One prompt covers several signatures in one step, for two minutes at most.

**Refuse** and **Halt** need no prompt, so you can stop a taf from a notification or a locked
phone. Neither can grant anything. Signing out forgets the key.

See also: [Held calls and approvals](/docs/approvals).
