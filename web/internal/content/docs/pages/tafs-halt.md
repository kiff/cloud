# Halt and resume

**Halt** stops one taf, or every taf with **Halt all**, from one screen.
For anyone who wants nothing more to happen until they've looked.
No run starts and no call reaches your tools; a call already on its way may still arrive.

The app shows **Halting…** until KIFF and planque, which keeps your connections, both confirm,
then **Halted**. If one doesn't confirm, **Halt again**.

**Resume** needs Face ID, and only new work runs. Halt is not **Pause** (stops the schedule) or
**Revoke** (ends the Card).
