# Credits and your balance

Each run of a taf uses **credits** for the AI it used.
For anyone who pays for tafs or watches its balance.
See what's left in **Plan & usage** in the app; prices are on [/pricing](/pricing).

## What a run uses

Whole credits, rounded up once per run, counted when the run ends. A refused call still used its
run's AI, so it still counts.

**Not counted:** Coach, enough to set up about 4 new jobs a month; trying a new version, which
sends nothing; a run that failed on our side.

## While your tafs wait

When the credits run out, no new run starts and a run already going finishes. A door's message or
an app's event is kept 48 hours and runs once your tafs go on, oldest first. Nothing is sent in
your business's name meanwhile.

On Company, someone who manages billing raises the spend limit in the app, with Face ID. Anyone
else can **Ask an admin**.

## Pricing details

**Solo**, for one person, is bought in the tafs app through the App Store or Google Play, VAT
included. It has 500 credits a month, then packs. **Company** is billed by seat on kiff.dev, with
500 credits a seat a month, pooled. A plan is billed in one place.

**Packs (Solo):** 500 credits €11.49, 1,500 credits €29.99, 5,000 credits €84.99. Bought in the
Android app today, not yet on the App Store or the web. They never expire, and are used after the
month's credits, oldest first.

**Company's extra credits:** €0.01 a credit, up to a monthly spend limit you set (€50 to start).
At the limit, your tafs wait. You're told at 80% and at 100%, once a month each.

**KIFF actions (Company):** your own agents get 500 actions a seat, pooled, then €0.02 an action.
An action counts when KIFF allowed the call, it was sent to a tool that changes something, and it
worked. Refused calls, held calls you refuse, halts, failures, retries of the same call and reads
aren't counted. Actions don't stop at a limit.

**Never billed:** agents and tafs, Cards, connections, approvers, viewers and their devices, the
halt, receipts, and exporting your data when you leave.

**VAT:** Company is priced without VAT, added where it applies; a business with an EU VAT number
outside Estonia is invoiced under reverse charge. Solo is priced as the stores sell it, VAT included.

**Committed volume:** negotiated prices on an order form. Write to gabriel@kiff.dev.
