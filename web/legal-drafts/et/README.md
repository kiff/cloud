# Estonian (et): legal texts (drafts)

Translations of the served English pages, for the countries whose language rule asks for the terms and privacy texts in
the local language (Estonia). The English text binds; each page says so at its top. Nothing here is served until
The site adds `et` and renders these files, byte-tested against them.
