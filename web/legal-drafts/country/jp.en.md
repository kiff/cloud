# Japan

This section applies if you live in Japan. It adds what the Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI) asks,
beyond KIFF's Privacy Policy and the tafs Privacy Policy.

## 1. Purposes of use

We use your personal information only to make and keep your account and sign you in, to do the jobs you hand to tafs,
to bill a paid plan, and to keep the service secure. If we ever want to use it for another purpose, we ask you first.

## 2. Providing it to third parties in foreign countries

Your personal information is provided to recipients outside Japan. For each country:

| Country | Recipients | Its personal-information system |
|---|---|---|
| Ireland, and the EU | Kiff Agents OÜ (Estonia), Amazon Web Services, Stripe | The EU's GDPR. Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission recognises the EU as having a system equivalent to Japan's. |
| United States | Clerk, Anthropic, Stripe, Resend, Cloudflare, Google, Apple, Composio | No single federal law; sector laws and state laws such as California's CCPA. The FTC can act against unfair or deceptive practices. |
| Other countries where Anthropic's models may run (Europe, Asia, Australia) | Anthropic | That country's law; the written agreement under "Our measures" applies wherever it runs. |

**Our measures.** Each recipient is bound by a written agreement to handle your personal information in line with the
APPI's standards: for the stated purpose only, kept secure, not passed on without the same terms, and deleted or
returned when the work ends. We check these measures regularly, and you can ask us for more about them.

**[legal-i18n]** Proposal: rely on these agreements (APPI Art. 28(1), "equivalent measures"), rather than consent,
because consent can be withdrawn and the service can't run without these recipients.

## 3. Security control measures

Each customer's data is kept apart in the database; it is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2 or later) and at rest; access
is limited to those who need it; and the key that approves actions stays on your own device. If a leak could harm your
rights and interests, we report it to the Personal Information Protection Commission and tell you.

## 4. Your requests

You can ask us to disclose, correct, add to, delete, stop using, stop providing to third parties, or stop providing
abroad your retained personal data, and to disclose our records of providing it to third parties. Write to
privacy@kiff.dev. We answer without delay, and don't charge for it.
