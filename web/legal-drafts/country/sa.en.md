# Saudi Arabia

This section is published in English and Arabic. If they differ, the English text applies.

This section applies if you use KIFF or tafs in Saudi Arabia. It adds what the Personal Data Protection Law (Royal Decree M/19 of 2021) and its Implementing Regulation ask, beyond KIFF's Privacy Policy and the tafs Privacy Policy. Where this section says more, it applies; for everything else, those two policies apply.

## 1. Who is responsible

Kiff Agents OÜ, a company in Estonia, is the data controller of your account and sign-in data. For what tafs keeps for your company (each taf's runs, chats, reports and files), your company is the controller and we act as its processor. Sections 3 and 4 of each policy say which is which.

## 2. What we process and why

What we process, why, on what legal basis and for how long is in sections 4, 5 and 8 of KIFF's policy and sections 4, 8 and 9 of the tafs policy. We process your data to run your account and your tafs, to keep the service secure, and to meet our legal duties. Where a consent is the basis, such as for the AI in tafs, we ask for it separately and you can withdraw it in Account.

## 3. Transfers outside Saudi Arabia

KIFF is run from Estonia and hosted in Ireland. Everything you send us leaves Saudi Arabia. Some recipients are in the United States, as section 7 of each policy lists. Each transfer rests on the safeguards section 7 of each policy names.

## 4. Your rights

You can ask to be told how your data is processed and why, to see it and have a copy, to have it corrected, to have it destroyed when it is no longer needed, and to withdraw a consent you gave. Write to privacy@kiff.dev. We answer within 30 days.

## 5. Children

KIFF and tafs are not for anyone under 16.

## 6. Representative

**Representative in Saudi Arabia.** A controller outside Saudi Arabia that processes the data of people in Saudi Arabia appoints an authorised representative there and registers with the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA). We have no representative in Saudi Arabia today. We will name one here, as the contact point for SDAIA and for people in Saudi Arabia, before KIFF and tafs are offered in Saudi Arabia. A transfer of your data abroad follows SDAIA's Regulation on Personal Data Transfer outside the Kingdom.

If a breach of personal data could harm you, we tell the Saudi Data and AI Authority within 72 hours of finding out and, where the harm could be serious, you too.

## 7. Where to complain

You can complain to the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA).
