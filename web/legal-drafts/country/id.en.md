# Indonesia

This section is published in English and Indonesian. If they differ, the English text applies.

This section applies if you use KIFF or tafs in Indonesia. It adds what Law no. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection (UU PDP) asks, beyond KIFF's Privacy Policy and the tafs Privacy Policy. Where this section says more, it applies; for everything else, those two policies apply.

## 1. Who is responsible

Kiff Agents OÜ, a company in Estonia, is the data controller of your account and sign-in data. For what tafs keeps for your company (each taf's runs, chats, reports and files), your company is the controller and we act as its processor. Sections 3 and 4 of each policy say which is which.

## 2. What we process, why, and how long

What we process, why, on what legal basis and for how long is in sections 4, 5, 8 and 9 of each policy. We process your data to run your account and your tafs, to keep the service secure, and to meet our legal duties. Where a consent is the basis, such as for the AI in tafs, we ask for it separately and you can withdraw it in Account.

## 3. Transfers outside Indonesia

KIFF is run from Estonia and hosted in Ireland. Everything you send us leaves Indonesia. Some recipients are in the United States, as section 7 of each policy lists. Each transfer rests on the safeguards section 7 of each policy names.

## 4. Your rights

Under UU PDP you can ask for information about how your data is processed and why, ask to see and to have a copy of it, ask us to correct it, ask us to stop processing, delete or destroy it, withdraw a consent you gave, object to a decision made only by automated processing that has legal effect on you, ask for it in a portable form, and seek redress for a breach of the law. Write to privacy@kiff.dev. We answer within 3 working days of a request to withdraw consent and within 30 days of any other request.

## 5. A breach

If a failure to protect personal data happens, we tell the people affected and the authority in writing within 72 hours of finding out, as UU PDP asks, and say what was disclosed, when, how, and what we did.

## 6. Children

KIFF and tafs are not for anyone under 16. A child's data in Indonesia needs the consent of a parent or guardian, so we do not offer KIFF or tafs to children.

## 7. Language, and the electronic system register

This section and the Terms are also in Indonesian, because Indonesian law asks that an agreement with a party in Indonesia be in Indonesian. The English text applies where they differ. A private operator of an electronic system that serves users in Indonesia registers it with the ministry for communications and digital affairs. We have not registered KIFF or tafs there today. We will do so before they are offered in Indonesia, and say so here.

## 8. Where to complain

You can complain to the authority for personal data protection in Indonesia once it is set up, and to the ministry for communications and digital affairs.
