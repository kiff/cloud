# Jordan

This section is published in English and Arabic. If they differ, the English text applies.

This section applies if you use KIFF or tafs in Jordan. It adds what Personal Data Protection Law no. 24 of 2023 ask, beyond KIFF's Privacy Policy and the tafs Privacy Policy. Where this section says more, it applies; for everything else, those two policies apply.

## 1. Who is responsible

Kiff Agents OÜ, a company in Estonia, is the data controller of your account and sign-in data. For what tafs keeps for your company (each taf's runs, chats, reports and files), your company is the controller and we act as its processor. Sections 3 and 4 of each policy say which is which.

## 2. What we process and why

What we process, why, on what legal basis and for how long is in sections 4, 5 and 8 of KIFF's policy and sections 4, 8 and 9 of the tafs policy. We process your data to run your account and your tafs, to keep the service secure, and to meet our legal duties. Where a consent is the basis, such as for the AI in tafs, we ask for it separately and you can withdraw it in Account.

## 3. Transfers outside Jordan

KIFF is run from Estonia and hosted in Ireland. Everything you send us leaves Jordan. Some recipients are in the United States, as section 7 of each policy lists. Each transfer rests on the safeguards section 7 of each policy names.

## 4. Your rights

You can ask to see your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw a consent you gave. Write to privacy@kiff.dev. We answer within 30 days.

## 5. Children

KIFF and tafs are not for anyone under 16.

## 6. Transfers and breach

**Transfers and breach.** Data may leave Jordan on the safeguards the law asks for, which section 7 of each policy names. If a breach of personal data could harm you, we tell the authority and you without delay.

## 7. Where to complain

You can complain to the authority that Jordan's Personal Data Protection Law names for personal data, and to the Ministry of Digital Economy and Entrepreneurship.
