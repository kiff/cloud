# Canada

This section applies if you live in Canada. It adds what the Personal Information Protection and Electronic
Documents Act (PIPEDA) and, in Quebec, the Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private
sector (as amended by Law 25) ask, beyond KIFF's Privacy Policy and the tafs Privacy Policy.

## 1. Consent and purposes

We collect, use and disclose your personal information only for the purposes in the two policies: to make and keep
your account, to do the jobs you hand to tafs, to bill a paid plan, and to keep the service secure. You consent when
you sign up and tick the box. Sending anything to the AI also asks you first, and you can withdraw that in Account.
Withdrawing a consent the service needs means we can no longer provide that part of it; we tell you what stops.

## 2. Outside Canada

Your personal information is stored and processed outside Canada: in Ireland (our hosting), and in the United States
and the other countries `/subprocessors` lists. While it's there, the courts and authorities of those countries may
be able to get access to it under their laws. In each case a written agreement requires the recipient to protect it
at a level comparable to ours.

**Quebec:** before personal information about someone in Quebec leaves Quebec, we assess whether it will be
adequately protected, taking into account its sensitivity, the purpose, the safeguards, and the legal framework where
it goes. **[owner]** The assessment itself is a record we keep, not published; mac-local and legal-i18n draft it for
your signature.

## 3. Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, how we use it and to whom we have disclosed it, and
to correct it. In Quebec you can also ask us to stop disseminating it or to de-index it, ask for it in a structured,
commonly used technological format, and be told about, and comment on, a decision made only by automated processing.
tafs makes no such decision about you: a taf acts for the company that set it up, inside the Card that company
issued.

Write to privacy@kiff.dev. We answer within 30 days. If we refuse, we tell you why and how to complain.

## 4. Person in charge of protecting personal information

**[owner]** Law 25 makes the company's highest-ranking officer the person in charge unless that role is delegated in
writing, and asks us to publish their title and contact. Proposal: "[Title], Kiff Agents OÜ, privacy@kiff.dev."

## 5. Breaches

If a breach of security safeguards creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we tell you and the Office of the
Privacy Commissioner of Canada (in Quebec, the Commission d'accès à l'information) as soon as possible, and we keep a
record of every breach.

## 6. Children

KIFF and tafs are not meant for anyone under 16, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

## 7. Where to complain

- Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada: priv.gc.ca
- In Quebec, the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec: cai.gouv.qc.ca
- In Alberta and British Columbia, that province's Information and Privacy Commissioner.

## 8. French

In Quebec, this policy, the terms and the app are offered in French. **[legal-i18n]** fr-CA comes from our fr text,
with Quebec's usage (e.g. « courriel », « clavarder » where it applies) and Quebec's legal names.
