# Australia and New Zealand

This section applies if you live in Australia or New Zealand. It adds what Australia's Privacy Act 1988 (the
Australian Privacy Principles) and New Zealand's Privacy Act 2020 (the Information Privacy Principles) ask, beyond
KIFF's Privacy Policy and the tafs Privacy Policy.

## 1. Outside Australia and New Zealand

Your personal information is stored and processed outside your country: in Ireland (our hosting), and in the United
States and the other countries `/subprocessors` lists. Before it goes, each recipient agrees in writing to protect it
at a level comparable to these laws (Australia's APP 8; New Zealand's IPP 12). The EU's data-protection law also
applies to everything we process.

## 2. Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and to correct it. Write to privacy@kiff.dev. We answer
within 30 days (in New Zealand, within 20 working days). If we refuse, we tell you why and how to complain. We don't
charge for a request.

## 3. Using KIFF without your name

Where it's lawful and practical, you can deal with us without giving your name. An account needs an email address;
the sample data in tafs needs no account at all.

## 4. Breaches

If a breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we tell you and the Office of the Australian Information
Commissioner, or New Zealand's Privacy Commissioner, as soon as practicable.

## 5. Children

KIFF and tafs are not meant for anyone under 16, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

## 6. Where to complain

Write to us first, at privacy@kiff.dev. If you're not satisfied with our answer:
- Australia: the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, oaic.gov.au
- New Zealand: the Privacy Commissioner, privacy.org.nz
