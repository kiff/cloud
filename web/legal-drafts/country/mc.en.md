# Monaco

This section is published in English and French. If they differ, the English text applies.

This section applies if you use KIFF or tafs in Monaco. It adds what Monaco's Law no. 1.565 of 3 December 2024 on the protection of personal data asks, beyond KIFF's Privacy Policy and the tafs Privacy Policy. Where this section says more, it applies; for everything else, those two policies apply.

## 1. What we process, why, and how long

What we process, why, and for how long is in sections 4, 5 and 8 of KIFF's policy and sections 4, 8 and 9 of the tafs policy. In short: your account, your devices, what you write to Coach and your tafs and what your connected apps return while a taf works, billing details on a paid plan, and request logs. We delete each when its period ends, or when you delete your account.

## 2. Transfers outside Monaco

KIFF is run by Kiff Agents OÜ, a company in Estonia, and hosted in Ireland. Everything you send us leaves Monaco. Some recipients are in the United States: Clerk (sign-in), Anthropic (the AI models behind Coach and each taf), Stripe (payment on a paid plan), Resend (email notices), Apple and Google (push alerts and purchases), Cloudflare and Google Workspace (mail to a kiff.dev address) and Composio (managed connections). Each transfer is protected by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, as section 7 of each policy says.

## 3. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, object to or restrict its processing, ask for it in a portable form, and withdraw a consent you gave. Write to privacy@kiff.dev. We answer within 30 days.

## 4. Children

KIFF and tafs are not for anyone under 16.

## 5. Privacy contact

Privacy contact: the managing director, Kiff Agents OÜ, privacy@kiff.dev.

**Representative in Monaco.** We have no representative in Monaco today. We will name one here, as the contact point for the Autorité de Protection des Données Personnelles and for people in Monaco, before KIFF and tafs are offered in Monaco.

## 6. Where to complain

You can complain to the Autorité de Protection des Données Personnelles (APDP) of Monaco.
