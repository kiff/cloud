# Singapore

This section applies if you live in Singapore. It adds what the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA) asks, beyond
KIFF's Privacy Policy and the tafs Privacy Policy.

## 1. Purposes and consent

We collect, use and disclose your personal data only for the purposes in the two policies, with your consent when
you sign up and tick the box, or as the PDPA otherwise allows. You can withdraw consent at privacy@kiff.dev; we tell
you what withdrawing means for the service before we act on it.

## 2. Outside Singapore

Your personal data is stored and processed outside Singapore: in Ireland (our hosting), and in the United States and
the other countries `/subprocessors` lists. Each recipient is bound by a written agreement to protect it to a standard
comparable to the PDPA's.

## 3. Your rights

You can ask to see your personal data and how it has been used or disclosed in the past year, and to correct it.
Write to privacy@kiff.dev. We answer within 30 days, or tell you within that time when we will.

## 4. Data protection officer

**[owner]** The PDPA asks us to name a data protection officer and publish a business contact. Proposal: the same
person as the other countries' privacy officer, at privacy@kiff.dev.

## 5. Breaches

If a breach is likely to cause you significant harm, or affects 500 people or more, we tell the Personal Data
Protection Commission within 3 days of assessing it, and tell you if you are affected.

## 6. Children

KIFF and tafs are not meant for anyone under 16, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them.

## 7. Where to complain

Write to us first, at privacy@kiff.dev. You can also contact the Personal Data Protection Commission: pdpc.gov.sg.
