# Thailand

This section is published in English and Thai. If they differ, the English text applies.

This section applies if you use KIFF or tafs in Thailand. It adds what the Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019) (PDPA) asks, beyond KIFF's Privacy Policy and the tafs Privacy Policy. Where this section says more, it applies; for everything else, those two policies apply.

## 1. Who is responsible

Kiff Agents OÜ, a company in Estonia, is the data controller of your account and sign-in data. For what tafs keeps for your company (each taf's runs, chats, reports and files), your company is the controller and we act as its processor. Sections 3 and 4 of each policy say which is which.

## 2. What we process, why, and how long

What we process, why, on what legal basis and for how long is in sections 4, 5 and 8 of KIFF's policy and sections 4, 8 and 9 of the tafs policy. We process your data to run your account and your tafs, to keep the service secure, and to meet our legal duties. Where a consent is the basis, such as for the AI in tafs, we ask for it separately, in clear words, and you can withdraw it in Account as easily as you gave it.

## 3. Transfers outside Thailand

KIFF is run from Estonia and hosted in Ireland. Everything you send us leaves Thailand. Some recipients are in the United States, as section 7 of each policy lists. Each transfer rests on the safeguards section 7 of each policy names.

## 4. Your rights

Under the PDPA you can ask to access and to have a copy of your data, ask for it in a portable form and for us to send it to another controller, object to its processing, ask us to delete or destroy it or to make it unidentifiable, ask us to restrict its use, ask us to correct it, and withdraw a consent. Write to privacy@kiff.dev. We answer within 30 days.

## 5. A breach

If a breach of personal data puts you at risk, we tell the Office of the Personal Data Protection Committee within 72 hours of finding out and, where the risk is high, you too, with the steps we take.

## 6. Children

KIFF and tafs are not for anyone under 16.

## 7. Representative in Thailand

A controller outside Thailand that offers services to people in Thailand names a representative in Thailand in writing. We have no representative in Thailand today. We will name one here, as the contact point for the Office of the Personal Data Protection Committee and for people in Thailand, before KIFF and tafs are offered in Thailand.

## 8. Where to complain

You can complain to the Office of the Personal Data Protection Committee of Thailand.
