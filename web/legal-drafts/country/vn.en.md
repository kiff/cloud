# Vietnam

This section is published in English and Vietnamese. If they differ, the English text applies.

This section applies if you use KIFF or tafs in Vietnam. It adds what Decree no. 13/2023/ND-CP on personal data protection and the Law on Personal Data Protection of 2025 ask, beyond KIFF's Privacy Policy and the tafs Privacy Policy. Where this section says more, it applies; for everything else, those two policies apply.

## 1. Who is responsible

Kiff Agents OÜ, a company in Estonia, is the data controller of your account and sign-in data. For what tafs keeps for your company (each taf's runs, chats, reports and files), your company is the controller and we act as its processor. Sections 3 and 4 of each policy say which is which.

## 2. What we process and why

What we process, why, on what legal basis and for how long is in sections 4, 5 and 8 of KIFF's policy and sections 4, 8 and 9 of the tafs policy. We process your data to run your account and your tafs, to keep the service secure, and to meet our legal duties. We ask for your consent separately, in plain words and for each purpose, where consent is the basis, such as for the AI in tafs, and you can withdraw it in Account.

## 3. Transfers outside Vietnam

KIFF is run from Estonia and hosted in Ireland. Everything you send us leaves Vietnam. Some recipients are in the United States, as section 7 of each policy lists. Each transfer rests on the safeguards section 7 of KIFF's policy names.

## 4. Your rights

You can ask to know how your data is processed, to see it and have a copy, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, to withdraw a consent you gave, and to complain and seek damages. Write to privacy@kiff.dev. We answer within 72 hours of a request to withdraw consent, to delete or to restrict, and within 30 days of any other request.

## 5. Children

KIFF and tafs are not for anyone under 16.

## 6. Impact assessments

A company that processes the personal data of people in Vietnam files, with the Ministry of Public Security, an assessment of the processing and, if the data leaves Vietnam, an assessment of that transfer. We have filed neither today. We will file both, and say so here, before KIFF and tafs are offered in Vietnam.

## 7. Where to complain

You can complain to the Ministry of Public Security of Vietnam, which oversees personal data protection.
