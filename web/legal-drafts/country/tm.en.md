# Turkmenistan

This section is published in English and Turkmen. If they differ, the English text applies.

This section applies if you use KIFF or tafs in Turkmenistan. It adds what the Law of Turkmenistan "On Information about Private Life and its Protection" of 20 March 2017 asks, beyond KIFF's Privacy Policy and the tafs Privacy Policy. Where this section says more, it applies; for everything else, those two policies apply.

## 1. Who is responsible

Kiff Agents OÜ, a company in Estonia, is the data controller of your account and sign-in data. For what tafs keeps for your company (each taf's runs, chats, reports and files), your company is the controller and we act as its processor. Section 3 of each policy says which is which.

## 2. Your consent

We use your personal data only for what the two policies describe. Where the law needs your consent, we ask for it before we use your data, and you can withdraw it at any time. Withdrawing it doesn't affect what we did before.

## 3. Transfers outside Turkmenistan

KIFF is run from Estonia and hosted in Ireland. Everything you send us leaves Turkmenistan. Some recipients are in the United States, as section 7 of each policy lists. The law lets personal data go to a country that protects it adequately, and otherwise with the person's consent or under a treaty Turkmenistan is party to. We transfer your data on the safeguards section 7 of KIFF's policy names, and, where the law needs it, with your consent. **[owner: lawyer check that no copy has to be kept in Turkmenistan]**

## 4. Your rights

You can ask to know whether we hold data about you, to see it, to have a copy of it, to have it corrected or blocked, to have it deleted, and to withdraw a consent you gave. Section 9 of KIFF's policy and section 10 of the tafs policy list the rest of your rights. Write to privacy@kiff.dev. We answer within 30 days at the latest, and sooner where the law sets a shorter time.

## 5. Children

KIFF and tafs are not for anyone under 16.

## 6. Where to complain

You can complain to the state body that oversees the protection of personal data in Turkmenistan, or go to court. **[owner: lawyer check of the body's name]**
