# Brazil

This section applies if you live in Brazil. It adds what the General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018) and
the Digital Statute of Children and Adolescents (ECA Digital, Law 15.211/2025) ask, beyond KIFF's Privacy Policy and
the tafs Privacy Policy.

## 1. Legal bases

| What | Why | Legal basis (LGPD Art. 7) |
|---|---|---|
| Your account and devices | To make your account, sign you in, check approvals | Performance of the contract (V) |
| What you write to Coach and your tafs, files, what your apps return | To do the jobs you hand to tafs | Performance of the contract (V); for the AI, also your consent (I), asked in the app |
| Billing details | To charge a paid plan | Performance of the contract (V); legal obligation (II) for tax records |
| Request logs | Security and abuse prevention; access logs kept as Brazil's Internet Civil Framework requires | Legitimate interest (IX); legal obligation (II) for access logs |

**Access logs.** As Brazil's Internet Civil Framework (Marco Civil, Art. 15) requires, we keep the access logs of
people in Brazil (the date, time and IP address of each access) for 6 months, kept secure and used for nothing else,
then delete them. **[mac-local]** Brazil's logs need the 6-month retention before Brazil is a market.

## 2. Age

In Brazil you must be at least 18 to use KIFF and tafs. KIFF and tafs are business tools, not directed at children or
adolescents, and we don't process their personal data.

## 3. Outside Brazil

Your personal data is processed outside Brazil: in Ireland (our hosting), and in the United States and the other
countries `/subprocessors` lists. Each transfer rests on the standard contractual clauses approved by the ANPD
(Resolution CD/ANPD 19/2024), or on the performance of the contract you asked for (LGPD Art. 33, IX).

## 4. Your rights

You can ask us to confirm we process your data; see it; correct it; anonymise, block or delete data that is
unnecessary or excessive; have it ported to another provider; delete data processed on your consent; know whom we
share it with; know what happens if you don't consent; withdraw consent; and ask for a review of a decision made only
by automated processing. tafs makes no such decision about you. Write to privacy@kiff.dev. We answer within 15 days.

## 5. Data protection officer (Encarregado)

The managing director, Kiff Agents OÜ, privacy@kiff.dev.

## 6. Where to complain

Write to us first. You can also contact the National Data Protection Authority (ANPD): gov.br/anpd.
