# Korea

This section applies if you use KIFF or tafs in the Republic of Korea. It adds what the Personal Information
Protection Act (PIPA) and the Act on the Development of Artificial Intelligence and the Establishment of Trust (the
AI Basic Act) ask, beyond KIFF's Privacy Policy and the tafs Privacy Policy. Where this section says more, it
applies; for everything else, those two policies apply.

## 1. What we process, why, and how long

| What | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Your account: the email address you sign in with, your name, and the identity from Apple or Google when you sign in with one of them | To make and keep your account and sign you in | Until you delete your account |
| Your devices: each device's public key, name, platform and dates, and a push token if you turn alerts on | To check that an approval came from your device, and to send alerts | The push token until you sign out or remove the device; the rest until the account is deleted |
| What you write to Coach and your tafs, the files you attach, and what your connected apps return while a taf works | To do the jobs you hand to tafs | Runs and chats 30 days; what your apps return only until the run ends; memory notes until the taf is removed |
| Billing details on a paid plan | To charge for the plan | As long as tax and accounting law requires |
| Request logs: IP address, browser and device, and the page or call | Security and abuse prevention | Up to 90 days; service logs 30 days |

The full list, with every retention period, is in section 8 of each policy.

**When we destroy it.** When a period ends, or you delete your account, we delete the data. Electronic records are
deleted so they can't be restored; the encrypted backups that hold a copy roll over within 7 days (KIFF's database)
or 35 days (the stores of tafs and planque). We keep nothing on paper. Where a law makes us keep a record longer, such
as an invoice, we keep it apart and use it for nothing else.

KIFF is run by Kiff Agents OÜ, a company in Estonia. Everything you send us leaves Korea. We transfer it because the
service can't run without it: it's needed to provide the service you signed up for, and we tell you here, as PIPA
Article 28-8(1)(3) provides.

**[owner]** PIPA asks for each recipient's contact for privacy questions. Proposal: link each name to its public
privacy page, as `/subprocessors` lists them, rather than copying addresses that change.

**You can refuse.** You can ask us to stop transferring your personal information outside Korea, at
privacy@kiff.dev. Because the service runs only through these recipients, stopping means we can no longer provide it,
and we delete your account as section 1 says.

## 3. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or stop the processing of your personal information, ask for it in a portable
form, and withdraw a consent you gave. You can also refuse a decision made only by automated means that significantly
affects your rights, or ask us to explain it. tafs makes no such decision about you: a taf acts for the company that
set it up, inside the Card that company issued.

Write to privacy@kiff.dev, or someone you authorise can write for you. We answer within 10 days. If we can't do what
you ask, we tell you why and how to object.

## 4. Children

KIFF and tafs are not for anyone under 16. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.
If we learn we have, we delete it.

## 5. Security

Our measures are on the security page. They include keeping each customer's data apart in the database, TLS 1.2 or
later in transit, encryption at rest, access only for those who need it, and a signing key that approves actions on
your own device and never leaves it. If a breach affects you, we tell you without undue delay.

## 6. Cookies

KIFF's site uses only the cookies needed to sign you in and keep your choices. You can block them in your browser;
signing in then won't work. The cookie policy lists each one.

## 7. Privacy officer

**[owner]** PIPA asks us to name a person responsible for privacy: a name or position, and a contact.
Proposal: "Privacy officer: [name, position], Kiff Agents OÜ, privacy@kiff.dev."

## 8. Domestic agent

PIPA and the AI Basic Act require a domestic agent in Korea only from certain thresholds (for example, more than one
million users in Korea on average a day, or revenue above those set by decree). We are below them, so we have none
today. We will name one, here, if that changes.

## 9. AI in tafs

tafs is built on generative AI. Coach and each taf are AI agents, run on Anthropic's models: what they write is
generated by AI. The app asks you before anything goes to the AI, and you can withdraw in Account. A message a taf sends to someone outside your company says it comes from an AI agent.
