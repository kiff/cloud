# United States

This section applies if you live in the United States. It adds what US state privacy laws ask, including the
California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, beyond KIFF's Privacy Policy and the tafs Privacy Policy.

## 1. Notice at collection

| Category (California's names) | What it is for us | Why we collect it | Sources | Who receives it | How long |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Identifiers | Email address, name, the identity from Apple or Google, account and device ids, IP address | To make your account, sign you in and keep it secure | You; Apple or Google when you sign in with them | Our service providers (`/subprocessors`) | Until you delete your account; IP address in logs up to 90 days |
| Commercial information | Your plan, what you bought, usage counts | To provide and bill the plan | You; Stripe; Apple and Google for in-app purchases | Stripe, Apple, Google | As tax and accounting law requires |
| Internet or other electronic activity | Request logs: browser, device, pages and calls | Security and abuse prevention | Your browser and device | AWS (hosting) | Up to 90 days; service logs 30 days |
| Sensitive personal information: account log-in | Your sign-in and session | To sign you in | You | Clerk (sign-in) | While your account exists |
| Customer content | What you write to Coach and your tafs, files, what your apps return to a taf | To do the jobs you hand to tafs | You; the apps you connect | Anthropic (AI models), AWS | Runs and chats 30 days; Anthropic up to 30 days |

We use sensitive personal information only to provide the service you asked for, as California's rules allow, and
not to infer anything about you.

## 2. We don't sell or share

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We have
not done so in the past 12 months. We honour the Global Privacy Control signal as an opt-out request; since we
neither sell nor share, there is nothing further to switch off. We have no actual knowledge of selling or sharing the
personal information of anyone under 16.

## 3. Your rights

Depending on your state, you can ask to:

- know what personal information we collect, use and disclose, and get a copy;
- correct it;
- delete it;
- opt out of sale, sharing, targeted advertising and profiling with legal or similarly significant effects (we do
  none of these);
- limit our use of sensitive personal information (we already use it only as section 1 says).

Write to privacy@kiff.dev, or delete your account in the tafs app (Account → Delete account). An authorised agent
can ask for you, with your signed permission. We check that a request comes from you, by the email address on the
account. We confirm within 10 business days and answer within 45 days; if we need longer, up to 45 days more, we tell
you why. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

If we refuse your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer, and we answer the appeal within 45 days. If you
live in a state whose law gives you that right and you disagree with the outcome, you can contact your state's
Attorney General.

## 4. Children

KIFF and tafs are not directed to children under 13, and not meant for anyone under 16. We don't knowingly collect
personal information from children under 13. If we learn we have, we delete it.

## 5. AI

Coach and each taf are AI agents, run on Anthropic's models, and what they write is generated by AI. A message a taf
sends to someone outside your company says it comes from an AI agent.
