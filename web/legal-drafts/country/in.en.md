# India

This section applies if you live in India. It adds what the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and
its Rules ask, beyond KIFF's Privacy Policy and the tafs Privacy Policy. **[mac-local]** Check which of the Rules'
phases are in force today; the text below is written for all of them.

## 1. Notice

We process your personal data, with your consent, for these purposes:

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Your account: email address, name, and the identity from Apple, Google or GitHub | To make and keep your account and sign you in |
| Your devices: public key, name, platform, dates, push token | To check approvals come from your device, and to send alerts |
| What you write to Coach and your tafs, files, what your apps return | To do the jobs you hand to tafs |
| Billing details on a paid plan | To charge for the plan |
| Request logs | Security and abuse prevention |

You can withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it: in Account (for the AI) or at privacy@kiff.dev
(for everything else). Withdrawing doesn't affect what was done before. Where the service needs that data, it stops
for you, and we tell you what stops before we act. You can also give, manage or withdraw consent through a consent
manager registered with the Data Protection Board of India.

## 2. Age

In India you must be at least 18 to use KIFF and tafs. We don't process the personal data of anyone under 18.

## 3. Outside India

Your personal data is processed outside India: in Ireland (our hosting), and in the United States and the other
countries `/subprocessors` lists, except any country the Government of India restricts.

## 4. Your rights

You can ask for a summary of your personal data and how we process it, and the names of those we shared it with; ask
us to correct, complete, update or erase it; and nominate someone to act for you if you die or can't act. Write to
privacy@kiff.dev. We answer within 90 days.

## 5. Grievances

The person who answers questions about your personal data: the managing director, Kiff Agents OÜ,
privacy@kiff.dev. Write to them first. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of
India.

## 6. Breaches

If a breach affects your personal data, we tell you and the Data Protection Board of India without delay.

## 7. Languages

You can ask for this notice in English or in any language in the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution of India.
**[legal-i18n]** Hindi first, on request.
