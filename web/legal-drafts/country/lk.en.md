# Sri Lanka

This section is published in English, Sinhala and Tamil. If they differ, the English text applies.

This section applies if you use KIFF or tafs in Sri Lanka. It adds what the Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022 (PDPA), as amended by Act No. 22 of 2025, asks, beyond KIFF's Privacy Policy and the tafs Privacy Policy. The Act's main provisions start on 1 January 2027; we follow them from today. Where this section says more, it applies; for everything else, those two policies apply.

## 1. Who is responsible

Kiff Agents OÜ, a company in Estonia, is the data controller of your account and sign-in data. For what tafs keeps for your company (each taf's runs, chats, reports and files), your company is the controller and we act as its processor. Section 3 of each policy says which is which.

## 2. What we process and why

What we process, why, on what legal basis and for how long is in sections 4, 5 and 8 of KIFF's policy and sections 4, 8 and 9 of the tafs policy. We process your data to run your account and your tafs, to keep the service secure, and to meet our legal duties. Where a consent is the basis, such as for the AI in tafs, we ask for it separately and you can withdraw it in Account.

## 3. Transfers outside Sri Lanka

KIFF is run from Estonia and hosted in Ireland. Everything you send us leaves Sri Lanka. Some recipients are in the United States, as section 7 of each policy lists. The PDPA lets personal data go abroad only on the basis the Act and the Data Protection Authority's directives set, such as a recognised level of protection or appropriate safeguards. We transfer your data to Estonia, Ireland and the United States on the safeguards section 7 of KIFF's policy names: the standard contractual clauses of the European Union, supplementary measures, and, for United States recipients that have certified, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. We keep the contracts that show them.

## 4. Your rights

You can ask to see your data, to have a copy of it, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to object to its processing, and to withdraw a consent you gave. Section 9 of KIFF's policy and section 10 of the tafs policy list the rest of your rights. Write to privacy@kiff.dev. We answer within 30 days at the latest, and sooner where the law sets a shorter time.

## 5. Children

KIFF and tafs are not for anyone under 16.

## 6. Where to complain

You can complain to the Data Protection Authority of Sri Lanka.
