# Glossary terms (a copy)

The Terms table of `kiff/tafs/docs/languages.md`, the one glossary, copied here because KIFF's tests
can't read kiff/tafs. `glossary.sha256` is the digest of its `|` rows, each ending in a newline, as
kiff/tafs computes it (`kiff/tafs/docs/glossary.sha256`): when the glossary changes, both files here are
updated together, and `TestGlossaryCopyIsCurrent` fails until they agree.
